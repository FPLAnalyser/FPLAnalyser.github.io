The bars are gone. These read as one table you scan down, with every club in it, rather than twenty cards you scroll past. All 20 clubs; Coventry, Hull and Ipswich have no Premier League attack/defence rating yet, so their rows are marked and estimated — the live page has to say that rather than quietly rank them.

What changed. Previously each club got a card with its own mini chart, which is a team-level read — fine for "does my guy have a good run", useless for "who is best in the league". A ranking is a comparison, so the whole league has to be on screen at once and every row has to be the same shape. D and E answer the other question: what these look like with more in them.
