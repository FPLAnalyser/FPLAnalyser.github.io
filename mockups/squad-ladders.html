Concept 1 — the squad ladder

Same grid, switchable metric

Clean sheets, projected goals, goal involvement, expected points and minutes are the same shape of question — this player, this week — so they should be the same grid, not five screens. The bottom row totals the squad for that week.

Metric
Shade

Below his average above
Why two shading modes. A keeper's xG next to a striker's is a meaningless comparison, so shading a whole grid on one absolute scale would paint every defender red and say nothing. Vs his own average asks the planning question — which of his six weeks are the good ones — and keeps the cross-player comparison in the right-hand column, where it belongs. Across the squad is the honest choice for clean sheets and minutes, which really are comparable between players. Switch between them on the clean-sheet tab and the grid tells you two different true things.

More to draw

Other ideas, and whether the data exists

Projected vs actual, cumulative

Your squad's running expected points against what it really scored. The gap is the honest answer to "am I unlucky or wrong", and it is the natural spine of the GW Review page.

Needs a per-gameweek projection log — start before GW1 or it cannot be reconstructed

Minutes risk strip

P(60+) per player per week, same ladder. The quietest way to lose a gameweek is four players on 60 minutes, and no page on the site shows it.

Data ready — it is the fifth tab above

Bench cost

Points left on the bench each week, and what an alternative bench order would have saved. Turns a vague regret into a number.

Needs played gameweeks

Effective ownership swing

For each of your players, the points you gain or lose against the field given how widely they are owned. Rank is what you actually play for.

Ownership is in ratings.json today

Fixture turn map

When each club's run flips from hard to kind, drawn as a single timeline. Answers "who do I buy in three weeks", which the current grid makes you work out by eye.

Data ready

Chip windows

Bench Boost, Triple Captain and Free Hit each scored across every remaining week, so the best window is a peak rather than a hunch.

Data ready — needs the optimal-stopping rule from the moneyball mockup

Price-change watch

Who in your squad is closest to rising or falling. The one genuinely time-sensitive thing FPL managers act on daily.

Not modelled — needs transfer-delta history the API does not expose directly

Team-vs-team correlation

How much of your squad rides on a single result. Already half-built as the defensive concentration chart; the general form covers attack too.
