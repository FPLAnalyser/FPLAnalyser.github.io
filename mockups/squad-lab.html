Two things to live with

Your own players meet twice, costing about 1.4 across the season. Six of fifteen are template at 17% average ownership — a differential side, which cuts both ways.

Where I'd go. L1 is the smallest change and fixes the actual complaint — every headline becomes readable and every number keeps its place. L2 is the strongest if the question is "what should I do", because it answers it before you tap anything; the cost is that five of six reads become one-liners. L3 keeps the grid you have and gives each tile a picture, which is the most work for the least change in what the page says. L4 is the boldest — it stops being a dashboard and becomes a to-do list — and it is the one that would make somebody screenshot it.

L1 and L2 combine well: the priority card on top, rows underneath instead of minis. That is probably the version to build.
