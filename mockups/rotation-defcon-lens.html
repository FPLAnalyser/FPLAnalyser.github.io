Def Con in the rotation planner, and what the board should rate on

Real 2026/27 fixtures, the site's own difficulty, and real Defensive Contribution scores from the ratings pipeline. Sixteen of the twenty clubs have at least one 70+ DC player.

1 · Surfacing Def Con

Why it belongs here. A rotation is a choice of clubs, and DC points arrive whether or not the clean sheet does — so two clubs with identical fixtures are not identical buys if one of them has Lacroix or Tarkowski in it. Nothing on the planner says so today.

2 · What the board rates on

The case for a toggle. The board ranks and rings on difficulty, which is a blend. If you are rotating defenders you want the clean-sheet projection; if you are rotating attackers you want goals. Both are already computed for the Goals & Clean Sheets tab — the planner just never asks for them.
