Concept 2 — goal threat

Where the goals are projected to come from

Projected xG for the whole squad, split by the line it comes from. The total is what the squad should score; the split is how exposed you are if one line goes quiet.

DEFMIDFWD
Reads as a balance check. The squad total barely moves — 2.59 to 2.83 xG a week — but the mix swings: midfield leads in GW2 (1.20 against the forwards' 1.19) and is well behind by GW5 (0.80 against 1.49). A squad whose threat keeps migrating between lines is one where captaincy should migrate too.
Concept 3 — variance

Floor and ceiling, not just the projection

The same six weeks simulated 60,000 times each. The line is the median week; the bands are the middle half and the 10th-to-90th range.

The honest one, and the one no competitor shows. The median GW1 is 60 points — but the 10th-to-90th range is 46.8 to 73.8, a 27-point spread on an unchanged squad. Six weeks of medians only fall from 60.0 to 56.1, a smaller move than a single week's noise. It reframes a bad gameweek as the range working normally rather than the plan being wrong, which is exactly the moment managers take a −4 they don't need.
