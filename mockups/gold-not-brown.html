Black and gold, without the brown

You are right, and it is measurable. Three separate things are making that cast, and only one of them is the gold itself.

Every hex below is computed, not eyeballed — composited alpha, and contrast ratios checked against the ground they sit on

Diagnosis

Where the brown actually comes from

R−B is how much redder than bluer a colour is. A true grey is 0. Every surface token on the site is positive, and the text is strongly so.

1 · The greys are warm. Mild on its own, but it is on every panel, every border and every caption at once, so the whole page sits under a beige film.
2 · The body text is cream, not white. #f4efe3 is a 42° hue at 44% saturation — that is a deliberate warm cream, and it is the single largest area of colour on any screen. Text is most of what you look at, so this is doing more work than the panels are.
3 · And the actual brown is the gold wash. The selected plan chip is filled with rgba(201,162,39,.14). Over the warm panel that composites to #2c2513 — hue 43°, saturation 40%. That is not gold and it is not black. It is brown, by arithmetic. Gold at 14% over near-black is mud by definition: you cannot dilute a mid-tone into a dark ground and get a metal, you get its earth pigment.

Neutralising the greys moved it by one tenth of one percent. Removing the wash took it to nothing — and the gold count is unchanged in all three, so the accent lost nothing.
So B barely helps, and that is the finding. Neutralising the greys moves the chip from #2c2513 to #2c2617 — three points of blue. The panel around it is cleaner, but the brown patch you were pointing at is still brown, because the wash is what makes it. C is where it goes away: the fill becomes a neutral lift and the gold does its job at full strength on the border, the tick and the name, which is where a metal reads as a metal.

If you want it

What changing this actually costs

Small, and contained, because everything reskins from the token block at the top of index.css.

ChangeWhereRisk
Neutral greys — 9 surface and line tokensindex.css :root dark blockLow. Contrast improves slightly on every pair I checked.
Neutral ink — 3 tokenssame blockLow. Body text goes 17.25:1 → 17.84:1 on the ground.
Drop the gold wash — --accent-softsame block, one valueLow, but it is used in ~30 places, so worth one pass with fresh eyes.
Brighter gold — 3 accent tokensaurum blockMedium. Share images, the splash and the favicon are drawn in the old gold and would need re-exporting to match.
Light modelight blockSeparate pass — its greys are warm for the same reason and want the same treatment.
My recommendation is C. Neutral greys, neutral ink, and kill the wash — that is three edits in one file, no re-exporting, and it removes the brown at its source. Keep #c9a227: it is the brand gold, it is on every share card, and once it is no longer sitting on a beige page and no longer being smeared at 14% opacity, it reads as gold. Take D only if, having lived with C, you still want the accent itself brighter — and budget an afternoon for the artwork if so.
