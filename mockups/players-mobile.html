The Players leaderboard on a phone

Real 2026/27 ratings. Today the table is 776px wide inside a 368px column: 4GW Rating, Pts, PPG and xPts are entirely off-screen behind a hidden scrollbar, and the Season rating badge is clipped at the right edge.

The constraint. Nine columns will not fit 390px and no amount of shrinking changes that. So the question is not how to compress the table — it is which two or three numbers earn a place in the row, and where the rest go. All three options below fit without a sideways scroll.

A · Two numbers, rest on tap

The one I would build. The row carries the season rating and the 4GW form rating — the two the page exists for — with position, club and price under the name. Tapping opens points, PPG, ownership and price in place. Nothing is hidden behind a scrollbar; it is behind a tap, which people find.
