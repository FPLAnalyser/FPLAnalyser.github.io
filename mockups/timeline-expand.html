Everything above is real: the fifteen is a plan built in the live builder, the fixtures and difficulty are this season’s, and the projections and hard counts are the numbers the app produces for that squad. Only the two “played” weeks are invented, because the season has not started.

What the expand actually costs to build: nothing new. The bars are the gameweek strip that already exists. The rows are the Fixtures tab, already built, re-drawn on the strip’s columns instead of its own. The footer rows are the projection and the transfers per week, which the strip already computes. It is one grid with a height animation on the rows.
