Best runs, rethought — and the rotation planner turned vertical

Real 2026/27 fixtures and the site's own 1dp difficulty throughout.

1 · Best runs

What is wrong now. The board invents a "score" where higher is better, on a page where every other number is difficulty and lower is better — so a 4.20 looks like a brutal run and is in fact the kindest. It is also unsortable on a phone, and it silently shows one run per club chosen by a rule the reader cannot see. All three options below drop the invented number and rank on difficulty itself.

2 · Rotation planner

What is wrong now. The strip shows one starter per gameweek even when you have asked to start three of five, and it names the club you would field without ever naming who they play — which is the thing that made it the pick. Turning the board vertical fixes both: gameweeks run down, your rotation clubs are the columns, and every cell can carry an opponent because the column is as wide as the phone divided by the group.
