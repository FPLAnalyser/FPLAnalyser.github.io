The Review is the Preview, one week later

Four sets of mockups invented a new page. The site already has the page — it just points the other way. GW Preview opens on the round at a glance, then Captain, then every fixture as an xG bar with clean-sheet percentages, then expected points, then who the fixture suits, then team news. The Review is that, in past tense, section for section, on the same components. Everything below is measured GW1.

Why this rather than another four directions. The Preview is the most finished page on the site and readers already know how to read it. A Review that shares its skeleton needs no learning, reuses its components, and makes the pair legible as a pair — you looked at the bar before the round and you look at the same bar after it. The one section with no honest past tense is Into the next one, and it is a link rather than a panel.

What the model said about them
3.16 average projection across the ten

They averaged 13.8, on mean ownership of 2.9%. The model had ranked them 7th, 18th, 34th, 36th, 67th, 70th, 141st, 167th, 190th — and 495th of 495. It had four of the ten in its own top forty and put Mendy dead last in the league. That is less a bad model than the shape of the game: a projection names a median, and the players who win a gameweek are the ones who beat theirs.

Where the model missed
1.66 mean absolute error over all 495 it projected · mean error +0.22

Sixteen players it rated 3+ did not play at all. Forty-one it rated under 1.5 played the hour and took 122 points between them. It missed on minutes, not on goals. The full report card →
