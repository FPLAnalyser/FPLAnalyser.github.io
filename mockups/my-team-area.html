My Team, as a place you come back to

Everything below is drawn on the real projection files in site_data/2026-27/ — the same 495-player GW2 snapshot the site would freeze at Friday's deadline. Where a number cannot exist yet, it is underlined like this and labelled. GW1's first match kicks off tonight, so nobody has scored a point in this season: every "actual" on this page is a shape, not a result.

The proposal is four tabs, not six. You listed live gameweek, planner, look back, season report, insights, and actions. Two of those are not tabs. Insights and actions have no home of their own — an insight about next week belongs in the planner, an insight about last week belongs in the review, and an action belongs wherever the thing it acts on is. Give them their own tab and every other tab becomes a place you look at numbers and leave. Spread them through, and each tab answers "so what".

And the tabs know what day it is. A manager's week has a shape — pick the team, watch it, count the damage, plan the next one — so the page opens on the tab that matches the moment rather than making you find it. The bar below shows what that looks like on the Friday of GW2, which is a week from now: deadline imminent, so Now leads.

07

Your season in one sentence, written weekly

Not a paragraph of generated filler. One claim, derived, that changes when the evidence does — and is allowed to be unflattering.

Your defence is the best-projected part of your squad and you keep benching the wrong half of it.

Assembled from a small set of tested claims with thresholds, not free text. If none of them clears its threshold, the space stays empty — which is the part that makes it worth reading.

08

The tab bar is a clock

The structural idea underneath all of this, and the cheapest thing here to build.

Tue–Fri opens on Plan — the deadline is far enough away to think
Fri, −6h opens on Now — team sheet, with the checklist
Sat–Mon opens on Now, live face — banked, in play, to come
Mon, late opens on Review — the actuals have landed

Every tab stays reachable. The page just stops asking you to pick the one the calendar already implies — and the deadline it reads is the same availability.json event the advance job uses.

What is real on this page and what is not.
