The sticky bar is the one that serves the stated job. People build a draft and want it rated — so while they are twenty rows into the market list, the rating and the projection stay on screen and move as they swap. Every edit gets an answer without a scroll.

The delta

What is actually new

Moved, not built
SquadRatingSheet out of its modal and under the board. SquadLab's contents become the six tiles. SquadInsights and TeamPlanning — 424 lines already written, currently mounted nowhere — become two of them.
New, small
The home verdict card and its "three things to fix", reusing the builder's verdict component. One branch on "have we got fifteen". The sticky bar on the builder.
New, needs data
"Better than 78% of squads built here." Needs a distribution: run the existing rating over a few thousand generated legal squads once, ship the percentile curve as a static file. It is the line that turns a rating into a verdict.

Nothing above needs a new model. The only genuinely new number is the percentile, and the only genuinely new layout is a card that already exists in another shape.
